# Personal Ledger Video Script

Target length: 2 minutes 20 seconds to 2 minutes 45 seconds. App-only capture at 16:9. No browser chrome, tabs, addresses, notifications, private paths, or cursor wandering. All visible records are fictional.

Voice reference for a later authorized narration pass: **Veda Sky — Friendly, Warm and Clear**. Do not generate or upload narration from this project.

## 0:00–0:12 — Open

**Visual:** Clean full-app view. Hold on “Fictional demo” and Northstar Household.

**Narration:**

“This is Personal Ledger, a fictional demonstration of what becomes possible when a person and a WebMCP agent share a clear, carefully limited workspace. Every name, amount, date, and account label you see is invented. This is demonstration software, not financial advice.”

## 0:12–0:45 — Observe

**Visual:** Move from the fictional boundary to balance, income, expense, and net cash-flow figures. Then settle on Recent activity.

**Narration:**

“We begin with Observe. The person sees the month at a glance: synthetic balances, income, expenses, and recent activity. The agent can read the same bounded summary through one small tool. It cannot reach a bank, payment system, file, inbox, calendar, or external service.”

## 0:45–1:15 — Understand

**Visual:** Filter Recent activity to Groceries, then Expense. Clear the filters and move to Category plan.

**Narration:**

“Next is Understand. The transaction tool accepts validated date, category, and type filters, with a small result cap. The category view adds context without judging the person or offering recommendations. Because the fixture and the tool share one source of truth, the screen and the agent stay aligned.”

## 1:15–1:55 — Draft

**Visual:** Open Draft entry flow. Enter “Juniper Home Supply,” 42.99, Home. Save the demo draft. Show the draft status in the table.

**Narration:**

“Then Draft. A person can prepare a fictional entry, or ask an agent to create the same session-only draft with a strict schema. The amount is stored in cents. Categories are allowlisted. URLs, external targets, account-like identifiers, and unexpected fields are rejected. Nothing is posted, paid, emailed, uploaded, or saved outside this page session.”

## 1:55–2:25 — Prove

**Visual:** Clear drafts. Return to the clean table. Briefly show the WebMCP three-tool note and the footer boundary.

**Narration:**

“Finally, Prove. Clear the draft and the ledger returns to its clean fictional state. The complete WebMCP surface is only three tools: one summary read, one filtered list, and one ephemeral draft. A privacy scan, evaluation harness, tests, and secure headers verify that the boundary is part of the product—not just a promise.”

## 2:25–2:38 — Close

**Visual:** Return to the full-app overview and hold.

**Narration:**

“Personal Ledger shows the useful middle ground: the person stays in control, the agent gains just enough context to help, and the system remains fictional, local, and deliberately limited.”

## Capture notes

- Record at 1920×1080 or 1280×720, 30 fps.
- Hide browser chrome and macOS UI; capture only the app viewport.
- Use smooth, deliberate moves and one-second holds before and after interactions.
- No audio is required in the local visual asset. The approved voice reference is reserved for a separate authorized ElevenLabs pass.
- Do not publish the recording or thumbnail from this project.
